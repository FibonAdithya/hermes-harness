# Tags

The allowed page and note tags, one `- tag — meaning` line each. Only the
curator edits this file. At most 40 tags. `finance`, `investing`, `health` and
`people` mark a page private and are never removed or renamed.

- hermes — the Hermes assistant, its harness, cron jobs and skills
- droplet — the DigitalOcean droplet that runs Hermes
- box — the home coding box (tig-server) that runs tasks and nightly work
- cost — spend, prices, budgets and subscriptions
- people — people the owner knows or works with (private)
- investing — investments and portfolios (private)
- tig — the TIG fleet and its machines
- talos — Talos Linux and the Kubernetes setup on it
- fleet — fleet-wide operations, health checks and self-healing
- coding — coding agents, repos, tooling and development practice
- finance — money, banking, bills and taxes (private)
- travel — trips, bookings and travel plans
- health — health and medical matters (private)
